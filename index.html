About the concept

Built for clearer community reporting.

AyosBrgy is a proposed barangay-level service that makes it easier for residents, employees, commuters, and property owners to report community infrastructure concerns.

In the planned real-world workflow, the Barangay Secretary's Office reviews reports, then assigns them to the appropriate Kagawad or maintenance team. Issues on city- or nationally-owned roads may be endorsed to the relevant agency.

Questions and answers

Frequently asked questions

Who can report an issue?

The proposed service is intended for barangay residents and other people who encounter problems while passing through the area. This student demo accepts example reports only.

Do I need a photo to submit?

No. Photos are encouraged but optional. The form can check up to three JPG or PNG images, each no larger than 5 MB. In this prototype, image contents are not uploaded to a server.

How do I track my report?

Save the reference number shown after submission and enter it on the Track page. You can also try BRGY-2026-0001 as a sample.

Will a real barangay receive my report?

No. AyosBrgy is currently a classroom front-end prototype with browser-local demonstration data. No barangay is connected to it.

Privacy & demonstration notice: For classroom testing, please use fictional personal information only. Submitted report details, but not reporter name/contact information or photo contents, may be saved to this browser's local storage. These records do not sync between devices. This prototype does not authenticate staff or process real-world reports.
